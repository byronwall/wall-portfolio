Orbs is a quick reproduction of a ball-merging game my son liked. Drop matching balls into the box, combine equal powers of two, and work toward 32,768.

## The whole loop

Drag to position the next ball, then tap to drop it. Matching values merge into a larger ball. I kept the game small so the basic idea was playable first.
