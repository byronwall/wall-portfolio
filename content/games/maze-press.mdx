Take the long way through classic corridors, skirt shaded islands, or visit treasure chests in order. On Windy Ridge, arrows mark passages you can cross in only one direction.

Choose a woodland animal with **Character**. Use **Full view** for more room, or **Reset** to try the same maze again.

Choose **Play maze** to trace a corridor with your finger or mouse. You can also use the arrow pad or keyboard. Tracing can cover five squares at once. At a fork, choose the next square yourself. The guide can flag wrong turns, give a hint when asked, or leave you to explore. Open **My mazes** to resume an attempt in the same browser.

For paper play, print the maze and its answer key separately. Each sheet includes a code that brings back the same puzzle.
