Take the long way through classic corridors, skirt shaded islands, or visit treasure chests in order. Rabbit Burrow lets you hop between two matching holes. On Windy Ridge, arrows mark passages you can cross in only one direction.

Choose **Play maze** to trace a corridor with your mouse, use the arrow keys, or tap an adjacent cell. The mouse can cover five squares at once, but stops at forks. The guide can flag wrong turns, give a hint when asked, or leave you to explore. Open **Saved mazes** to resume an attempt in the same browser.

For paper play, print the maze and its answer key separately. Each sheet includes a code that brings back the same puzzle.
