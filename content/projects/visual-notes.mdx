Visual Notes started as a small markdown ingest app with an embeddings canvas. It has turned into the place where I keep notes, tasks, voice memos, research, and people, and where agents read and edit that information with me. It replaced two of my other projects: [Plantasktic](/projects/1-plantasktic) for tasks and [Video to Context](/projects/video-to-context) for voice memos.

- Source: [byronwall/visual-notes](https://github.com/byronwall/visual-notes)

## Interface

A voice memo recorded on a note. The audio is transcribed, then a planning pass writes a summary, splits the transcript into titled sections, and pulls out tasks with timestamps.

## What it does now

- **Capture.** A borderless editor with autosave, a slash menu, and a keyboard-driven notes list.
- **Voice memos.** Record on a note or drop memos in. Transcripts are searchable and turn into summaries, sections, and tasks.
- **Tasks.** Checkboxes in notes are real tasks, with lists, due days, and one inbox.
- **Collections.** Typed collections whose rows are notes, shown as a table, board, gallery, or calendar, and embeddable live in a note.
- **Research and pathways.** Research topics with streams, typed links, and comments addressed to the agent. Pathways are decision maps with lanes and voting.
- **People.** Profiles, organizations, relationships, and a reconnect queue.
- **Cleanup.** A daily card stack for revisiting and archiving old notes.
- **Search and canvas.** Full-text and embedding search together, and a UMAP canvas with labeled regions.
- **Agents.** An MCP connector covers notes, tasks, collections, research, pathways, people, events, and search. Agents can propose edits that I accept inline.

It moves fast: 766 commits on `main` in the last three months.

## How it works

Visual Notes is a SolidStart app on Prisma and PostgreSQL. Notes are markdown with block IDs, so an agent can edit one block without rewriting the note. OpenAI embeddings and UMAP drive the canvas, and Groq Whisper does the transcription.
