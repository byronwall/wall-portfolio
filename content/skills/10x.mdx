## What it does

I give it a target (a URL, a route, a component, a screenshot) and a sentence about who uses it. It comes back with something clearly better than a polish pass. It doesn't stop to ask questions. It makes the calls and lists them under "Decisions for you."

The point is structure. Restyling a bad layout gets you a nicer bad layout. 10x has to change what the user can do or see.

## How it works

1. **Ground.** Run the app, screenshot it at desktop and phone width, and walk the main job with real data. Note every place you had to hunt, scroll, or read raw JSON.
2. **Diagnose.** Score seven lenses from 0 to 10: job clarity, information architecture, density, disclosure, interaction, stability, fit and finish. The lowest two or three are where the 10x comes from.
3. **Ideas.** Write 8 to 12 ideas across remove, restructure, reveal, empower, and polish. At least four must be structural.
4. **Choose** a bundle with [consider-contrast](/skills/consider-contrast) and build it end to end.
5. **Verify** in the browser with before and after screenshots, then leave a handoff note for [100x](/skills/100x).

It reads [my UI taste](/skills/byron-ui-taste) and a list of the complaints I made after earlier passes, so it doesn't repeat them.

## What it made

The plans list in Planboard, before and after one pass.

![Planboard plans list before the 10x pass: long wrapped rows with a full next-action paragraph in its own column](/images/skills/10x-before.jpg)

![Planboard plans list after the 10x pass: one dense row per plan with a stage bar, milestone progress, and the next action on one line](/images/skills/10x-after.jpg)

*Before: each plan took four or five lines, and the next action was a paragraph in its own column. After: one row per plan, a stage bar, milestone progress, and the next action under the title. Almost twice as many plans fit on the screen.*

## It's working if

- The before and after screenshots look like different products.
- The main question gets answered in the first viewport.
- Something got deleted.
